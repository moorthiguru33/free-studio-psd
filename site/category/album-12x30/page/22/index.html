Free Photo Album 12x30 PSD Designs – Page 22

12x30 is a slightly shorter panoramic sheet than 12x36 and many labs print it at a lower price, which makes it popular for budget packages and parent albums. These files are built at true 12x30 proportions (for example 9000x3600 pixels at 300 DPI), so you do not have to crop or stretch a 12x36 layout.

Free Album Background PSD Designs – Page 3

Album backgrounds are the textures, patterns and soft scenes that sit behind your photo frames. A good background sets the colour mood of a spread without competing with faces, so most designs here keep the centre calm and put detail near the edges. They are prepared at album-sheet proportions (mostly 12x36) and high resolution, so they stay sharp in print. Pair one background family across a whole album for a consistent look, and use a Hue/Saturation adjustment layer if you need to match a client's colour theme. 773 editable Album Background PSD files, sorted by preview quality; download any design free.

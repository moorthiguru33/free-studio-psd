Telugu weddings are colourful and grand, from Pellikoothuru and Mangala Snanam to Jeelakarra Bellam and Talambralu. Royal, golden and ornate album layouts suit these ceremonies best, so this page focuses on those styles.

Why designers choose these telugu wedding album psd files

Every file on this page is a layered Photoshop document, not a flattened image. Photos sit inside clipping masks, decorations are grouped, and text layers stay editable, so you can adapt each design to a client in minutes. Unlike many free PSD sites, there is no ZIP password, no login and no signup: open a design, check the specifications, and download it from Google Drive.

Specifications at a glance

These telugu wedding album psd designs are prepared for 12x36 printing. Most files are 200 or 300 DPI in RGB colour, which is what most Indian digital labs expect. Check the dimensions on each design page before you start: a 12x36 spread at 300 DPI is 10800x3600 pixels, while at 150 DPI it is 5400x1800 pixels. Keep faces and names at least half an inch away from the centre fold and the trim edge.

How to edit a telugu wedding album psd file in Photoshop

1. Download the ZIP and extract it with your system tool or 7-Zip. 2. Open the PSD in Adobe Photoshop CC. 3. Drag your photo onto the document, place it directly above the frame layer and press Alt+Ctrl+G (Option+Cmd+G on Mac) to clip it. 4. Resize with Ctrl+T, then edit names and dates with the Type tool. 5. Recolour ornaments with Hue/Saturation adjustment layers so the original artwork stays untouched. 6. Save a flattened JPG at full resolution for the lab and keep the PSD for corrections.

Tips for a better telugu wedding album psd album

Plan the album in story order before you open Photoshop: preparations, ceremony, portraits, family and celebration. Give each spread one hero photo and two to four supporting images instead of filling every frame. Repeat a small colour palette across the book so pages flow together, and use backgrounds from the same set. Proof every spread at 100% zoom, and double-check the spelling of names and dates; clients notice these first.

Printing and file preparation

Ask your lab for its preferred format before export. Most labs accept high-quality JPG in sRGB; some prefer TIFF or a specific colour profile. Leave the bleed your lab recommends (usually 0.125 to 0.25 inch), flatten a copy for print and archive the layered PSD so reprints and client changes are easy.

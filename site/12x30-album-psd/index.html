12x30 vs 12x36

A 12x30 spread is 9000x3600 pixels at 300 DPI. It is slightly narrower than 12x36, which lowers printing cost and suits medium-sized albums. Compare both formats in our 12x30 vs 12x36 guide.

Specifications at a glance

These 12x30 album psd designs are prepared for 12x30 printing. Most files are 200 or 300 DPI in RGB colour, which is what most Indian digital labs expect. Check the dimensions on each design page before you start: a 12x36 spread at 300 DPI is 10800x3600 pixels, while at 150 DPI it is 5400x1800 pixels. Keep faces and names at least half an inch away from the centre fold and the trim edge.

How to edit a 12x30 album psd file in Photoshop

1. Download the ZIP and extract it with your system tool or 7-Zip. 2. Open the PSD in Adobe Photoshop CC. 3. Drag your photo onto the document, place it directly above the frame layer and press Alt+Ctrl+G (Option+Cmd+G on Mac) to clip it. 4. Resize with Ctrl+T, then edit names and dates with the Type tool. 5. Recolour ornaments with Hue/Saturation adjustment layers so the original artwork stays untouched. 6. Save a flattened JPG at full resolution for the lab and keep the PSD for corrections.

Tips for a better 12x30 album psd album

Choose layouts that match the photos you actually have. A spread with six small frames looks busy if your best images are wide shots. Keep one consistent font pairing across the album, and use the ornaments sparingly; hiding half the decorations often makes a template look more premium.

Printing and file preparation

Ask your lab for its preferred format before export. Most labs accept high-quality JPG in sRGB; some prefer TIFF or a specific colour profile. Leave the bleed your lab recommends (usually 0.125 to 0.25 inch), flatten a copy for print and archive the layered PSD so reprints and client changes are easy.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert a 12x36 design to 12x30?

Yes. Reduce the canvas width and move the outer frames inward. Avoid scaling the whole design unevenly, because that stretches photos and ornaments.

Are these 12x30 album psd files really free?

Yes. Every 12x30 album psd design on PSD Album is free to download. There is no login, no signup and no ZIP password.

What size are the 12x30 album psd designs?

They are prepared for 12x30 printing. The exact pixel dimensions, DPI and file size are listed on each design page.

Which software do I need?

Adobe Photoshop CC is recommended. Any editor that supports layered PSD files can open them, but clipping masks work best in Photoshop.

How do I replace the photos?

Place your photo above the frame layer and press Alt+Ctrl+G (Option+Cmd+G on Mac) to clip it into the mask, then resize it with Ctrl+T.

Can I use these designs for client albums?

Yes, you can use them for personal projects and studio client work. Reselling or re-uploading the PSD files as template packs is not allowed.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

This Privacy Policy explains how PSD Album (psdalbum.in, "we", "us") handles information when you visit the site. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not sell personal data.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.
  • Server and security logs: our hosting and network provider (Cloudflare) processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Analytics: we may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first service that does not use cookies and does not track you across sites, to count page views.
  • Data stored on your device: favourites, recently viewed designs and your theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. PSD Album itself does not need cookies to work. Third parties described below, mainly advertising partners, may place and read cookies or use similar technologies (such as web beacons and device identifiers) when you visit our pages.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use, or may use, Google AdSense and other third-party advertising partners to show ads. In particular:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick / DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices and, in Europe, at www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • If other ad networks are used, they may also use cookies and similar technologies under their own privacy policies.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may be shown a consent message through a Google-certified consent tool before personalised ads are used.

Downloads from Google Drive

Template files are delivered through Google Drive. When you open a download link you are on Google's service and Google's privacy policy applies.

Links to other sites

We link to other websites, including our sister site TamilPSD.in. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites.

Children

The site is meant for photographers and designers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Because we do not run accounts, the only personal data we normally hold is email correspondence; write to us and we will delete it on request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@psdalbum.in.

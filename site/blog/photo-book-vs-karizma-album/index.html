Photo Book vs Karizma Album: What Is the Difference?

Clients often ask whether they should choose a photo book or a Karizma album. Both hold printed photos in a bound book, but they differ in printing method, page thickness, durability and feel. Understanding the differences helps studios recommend the right option and price it fairly. Exact specifications vary by lab, so use this as a general guide and confirm details with your lab.

By Guru Moorthi · Updated 2026-10-21 · 3 min read

What people mean by a Karizma album

In India, 'Karizma album' is a widely used name for premium albums with thick, rigid pages, usually printed on photographic paper and often laminated, bound so that spreads open flat or nearly flat. The 12x36 spread is the most common size. They are designed to last for decades of handling.

What a photo book usually is

A photo book is typically printed on a digital press on coated paper, with thinner pages bound like a book. Photo books are lighter, often more affordable and come in many sizes, including small formats suitable for gifts.

Key differences

  • Printing: Karizma-style albums commonly use photographic printing; photo books commonly use press printing. Photographic prints usually give smoother tones and deeper colours.
  • Page thickness: Karizma pages are thick and rigid; photo book pages are thinner and flexible.
  • Lay-flat opening: Karizma albums are often lay-flat; many photo books have a visible centre gutter, although some lay-flat photo books exist.
  • Durability: thick laminated pages resist bending and fingerprints well; thinner pages can crease.
  • Weight and size: Karizma albums are heavy, especially at 12x36 with many sheets; photo books are lighter and easier to send.
  • Cost: Karizma albums usually cost more per page than photo books, but prices vary widely by lab.

Design differences

Lay-flat Karizma spreads allow panoramic photos across both pages, as long as faces stay off the exact fold. Photo books with a gutter need a wider safe zone around the centre, and panoramic images across the gutter should be avoided or positioned carefully. Photo books with more pages can hold more photos per album at a lower cost, which suits storytelling with many candid images.

Which to recommend

  • Main wedding album: Karizma-style albums are the traditional choice for durability and premium feel.
  • Parent or gift albums: smaller photo books or mini albums are cost-effective.
  • Pre-wedding or travel albums: photo books suit modern, story-like layouts.
  • Birthday and baby albums: either works; families who want many photos may prefer a photo book with more pages.

Preparing files

Both formats generally use sRGB JPG files, but page sizes, bleed and spine requirements differ. Download the lab's templates for the chosen product and follow its guides. For panoramic 12x36 albums, see the 12x36 album size guide; for paper choices, see choosing paper finishes.

Templates

Most album templates, including the Karizma album PSD collection, are designed for 12x36 lay-flat spreads. For photo books with a gutter, move frames slightly away from the centre and keep text further inside the safe area.

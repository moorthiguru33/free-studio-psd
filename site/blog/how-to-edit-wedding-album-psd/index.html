How to Edit a Wedding Album PSD in Photoshop (Step-by-Step)

Open the PSD and check the Layers panel. Album templates group photo placeholders, text and decorations separately.

Drag your photo onto the document and place it just above a placeholder layer, then press Alt+Ctrl+G (Option+Cmd+G on Mac) to clip it inside the mask.

Use Free Transform to scale and position each photo. Hold Shift to keep proportions.

Edit names and dates with the Type tool, and recolour shapes using Hue/Saturation adjustment layers.

Save a copy as JPG at full resolution for the print lab and keep the PSD for future edits.

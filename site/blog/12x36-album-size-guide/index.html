12x36 Album Size Guide: Pixels, DPI and Print Settings

A 12x36 inch spread at 300 DPI is 10800x3600 pixels; at 150 DPI it is 5400x1800 pixels.

Most labs accept 150–300 DPI. Higher DPI gives sharper prints but larger files.

Leave a 0.25 inch bleed and keep faces and text away from the centre fold.

Use RGB for most digital labs unless your printer asks for CMYK.

Wedding Album Sizes in India: Spread Sizes, Book Sizes and Pixels

Indian studios describe albums by the size of the open spread, so a '12x36 album' is a book that measures 12 x 18 inches when closed and 12 x 36 inches when opened flat. Confusing spread size with book size is one of the most common causes of wrong files at the lab. This guide lists the popular sizes and the pixel dimensions you need.

By Guru Moorthi · Updated 2026-10-21 · 3 min read

How album sizes are written

An album size such as 12x36 gives the height first and the full open width second. Each sheet you design is one spread: the left page and right page together, with the fold in the middle. A 12x36 spread folds at 18 inches, giving two 12x18 pages. Some labs write the closed book size instead, so always confirm which one they mean.

Popular sizes and pixel dimensions at 300 DPI

Pixels are simply inches multiplied by DPI. At 300 DPI:

  • 12x36 spread (12x18 book): 10800 x 3600 pixels. The most popular Karizma and wedding album size.
  • 12x30 spread (12x15 book): 9000 x 3600 pixels. Slightly more compact and often more economical.
  • 14x40 spread (14x20 book): 12000 x 4200 pixels. Used for premium, large-format albums.
  • 12x24 spread (12x12 square book): 7200 x 3600 pixels. Popular for pre-wedding and small albums.
  • 10x30 spread (10x15 book): 9000 x 3000 pixels. A lighter option for parent or reception albums.
  • 12x18 sheet: 5400 x 3600 pixels. Common for single pages, birthday albums and studio prints.
  • 18x24 sheet: 7200 x 5400 pixels. Used for portrait-format and poster-style sheets; confirm orientation with your lab.

At 200 DPI, which some labs accept, multiply inches by 200 instead; a 12x36 spread becomes 7200 x 2400 pixels. See photo print sizes in pixels and DPI for a full chart.

Choosing a size

  • Number of photos: larger spreads hold more photos without crowding. A 12x36 sheet comfortably fits one hero photo and several supporting frames.
  • Budget: cost rises with size, paper and sheet count. See Karizma album printing cost factors.
  • Handling: 14x40 albums are impressive but heavy; parents' albums are often smaller copies.
  • Style: square 12x24 books suit modern, minimal layouts; wide 12x36 spreads suit panoramic and traditional designs.

Our comparisons of 12x30 vs 12x36 and 12x36 vs 14x40 go deeper.

Bleed and safe area

Labs trim a small amount from every edge and lose some area into the binding. Keep faces and text a safe distance from the edges and well away from the centre fold. The exact bleed differs between labs, so ask for their specification sheet and add guides in Photoshop before you design.

Matching templates to your size

Use templates built for the size you print. A 12x36 template can be adapted to 12x30, but it needs reworking rather than simple scaling because the proportions differ; see how to resize an album PSD to another size.

Quick checklist

  • Confirm whether the lab means spread size or book size.
  • Confirm the DPI they require.
  • Set up the canvas at the exact pixel size and add fold and safe-area guides.
  • Export a test sheet before designing the whole album, and ask the lab to confirm it.

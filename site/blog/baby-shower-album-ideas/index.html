Baby Shower Album Design Ideas

Baby showers, known in many Indian families as Valaikappu, Seemantham, Godh Bharai or Simantonnayana, combine rituals, blessings and celebration. An album of the event is a meaningful keepsake for the mother-to-be and her family. Here are ideas for structuring and designing it.

By Guru Moorthi · Updated 2026-10-18 · 3 min read

Understand the ceremony

Customs vary by region and family. A Tamil Valaikappu centres on bangles being placed on the mother's wrists by married women of the family; a Godh Bharai involves filling the mother's lap with gifts and blessings; Seemantham ceremonies include specific rituals and prayers. Ask the family which moments matter most and in what order they happened, and use that as the album's structure.

Suggested structure

  • Opening spread: the mother-to-be's portrait with the event name and date.
  • Preparations: decor, outfits, flowers, bangles and gifts.
  • The main ritual in sequence: bangles, blessings, lap-filling or prayers.
  • Blessings from elders and relatives.
  • Portraits of the couple.
  • Family groups from both sides.
  • Celebration, food and guests.
  • Closing spread with a quiet portrait.

Design style

Baby shower albums suit warm, joyful but soft designs. Pastel pinks, peach, yellow, mint and gold accents work well, along with florals, bangles and gentle motifs. Keep areas behind faces clean. Our birthday and baby album category includes soft layouts that adapt easily to baby shower photos once you recolour the ornaments.

The bangle or blessing sequence

The central ritual usually involves many relatives performing the same action. Instead of giving each person a large frame, use sequence spreads with consistent frame sizes, and reserve one large frame for the most emotional moment, such as the mother's reaction or her mother's blessing.

Couple and maternity portraits

If the photographer captured maternity portraits of the couple, give them a minimal spread with large photos. These images often become the parents' favourites and work well as the album's opening spread.

Text and captions

Short captions in the family's language, the event name and the date add meaning. Use proper Unicode fonts for Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam or Hindi and have them proofread.

Size and pairing

Baby shower albums are usually shorter than wedding albums; 12x30 or 12x24 sizes work well. Many families later order a baby album after the birth. Designing both with the same fonts and a related palette creates a lovely set; see our baby album design guide.

Practical tips

  • Indoor home ceremonies often have mixed lighting; correct skin tones consistently.
  • Expect many group photos; plan family spreads in advance.
  • Keep the mother's face away from the centre fold.
  • Confirm names and spellings of relatives if you label family spreads.

Decor and gifts

Baby shower decor is often elaborate: flower swings, bangle stands, fruit and sweet trays, rangoli and themed backdrops. Photograph-heavy decor deserves a detail spread with several small frames, ideally placed right after the opening portrait so viewers see the setting before the ritual begins. Gifts and the traditional trays can share this spread.

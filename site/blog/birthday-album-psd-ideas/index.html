Birthday and Baby Album Ideas Clients Love

Birthday and baby albums are a big share of studio work after weddings, and they need a very different style: brighter, softer and more playful. Here are layout and story ideas that make these albums feel personal, plus tips for using birthday album PSD templates.

By Guru Moorthi · Updated 2026-10-19 · 3 min read

Build the album as a story

  • Opening spread: the child's name, age and a hero portrait.
  • Preparations: decorations, the cake, the venue being set up.
  • Arrival and dressing: the birthday outfit and close-up details.
  • Cake cutting: a sequence of three to five photos across one spread.
  • Games and friends: candid moments with cousins and friends.
  • Family: parents, grandparents and the extended family group.
  • Closing page: a thank-you note or a quiet portrait.

First birthday milestone pages

For first birthdays, many parents love a 'twelve months' spread with one photo from each month, along with the baby's weight, first word or first steps. Templates with a grid of small frames work well for this; keep the background simple so twelve photos do not feel busy.

Colour and theme

Match the album to the party theme: jungle, princess, cartoon, space or a pastel colour scheme. Pick two or three colours from the decorations and recolour template ornaments with adjustment layers so every spread uses the same palette. Avoid mixing many different template styles in one album.

Text that adds meaning

Short captions such as the date, the venue, 'First steps', or a line from the parents make the album more personal than decorative quotes. Use one playful font for headings and one clean font for small text.

Baby and newborn albums

Newborn albums need softer tones (cream, pastel pink, sky blue, mint) and larger photos with plenty of white space. Include details such as tiny hands and feet, the naming ceremony and the first family portrait. Baby shower spreads can lead into the newborn section to tell the whole story.

Working with templates

Choose birthday album PSDs where the frames match the orientation of your best photos, and hide ornaments that compete with the child's face. Keep faces away from the centre fold on 12x36 sheets, and check spelling of the child's name on every page. Browse our birthday album PSD collection for free 12x36 kids and first birthday designs.

Sizes and paper for kids' albums

Birthday albums are often smaller than wedding albums. 12x36 is still common for big first birthday functions, but many parents prefer a 12x30 or 12x18 book that is easier for grandparents to hold and less expensive to reprint. Matte or lustre paper suits pastel themes and hides fingerprints, which matters for an album children will handle. Ask the lab about thicker sheets or a protective laminate if the album will be passed around at family gatherings.

Frequently asked questions

Are these Pre-Wedding Album PSD files really free?

Yes. All 213 Pre-Wedding Album PSD designs on FreeStudioPSD are free to download with no login or signup.

Which software opens Pre-Wedding Album PSD files?

The files are layered PSDs. Open them in Adobe Photoshop CC or any editor that supports PSD layers.

How do I replace the photos?

Place your photo above the frame layer and press Alt+Ctrl+G (Option+Cmd+G on Mac) to clip it into the mask, then resize with Ctrl+T.

Can I use these designs for client albums?

Yes, you can use them for personal and studio client work. Reselling the files as template packs is not allowed.

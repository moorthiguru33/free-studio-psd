About PSD Album

PSD Album (psdalbum.in) is a free library of layered Photoshop templates for Indian photo studios, wedding photographers and album designers. It is run by Guru Moorthi, who has worked with album design and photo-studio workflows for years and started this site to make good starting templates easy to find without passwords, logins or survey walls.

What you will find here

The library covers 12x36 wedding album sheets, Karizma album designs, 12x30, 18x24 and 12x18 sheets, album covers, album backgrounds, studio backdrops, birthday and baby albums and wedding invitations. Every design has its own page with a full preview, the canvas size in pixels, the DPI, the file size and the category, so you can check that a file suits your print size before you download it.

How designs are checked

Before a design is published we generate a preview from the actual PSD, read its dimensions and resolution from the file, and remove exact and near-duplicate files so the library does not fill up with the same layout under different names. Titles and descriptions are written to describe what the design actually contains. If you find a broken download, a wrong size or a duplicate, please write to us and we will fix it.

Free, with no login

All files are free to download. There is no account, no signup and no ZIP password. Downloads are delivered from Google Drive.

How the site is funded

PSD Album is supported by advertising and by promoting TamilPSD Pro, our sister site with a larger premium PSD collection. Free downloads here never require a purchase.

Guides

Our album design guides cover album sizes, editing PSD templates in Photoshop, print preparation and running an album design business.

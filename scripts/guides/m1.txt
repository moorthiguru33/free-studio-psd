kg2lb
Kilograms vs pounds: where each is used
The kilogram is the SI unit of mass and is used for everyday weights in almost every country. The pound (avoirdupois) is common in the United States and is still used informally in the United Kingdom, often together with stone for body weight. Since 1959 the pound has been defined exactly as 0.45359237 kg, so conversions are precise rather than approximate.
Quick mental maths
To estimate pounds, double the kilograms and add 10%: 70 kg × 2 = 140, plus 14 gives about 154 lb (exact: 154.32 lb). To go the other way, halve the pounds and subtract 10%: 200 lb ÷ 2 = 100, minus 10 gives about 90 kg (exact: 90.72 kg). For airline baggage, 23 kg is about 50.7 lb and 32 kg is about 70.5 lb – the two most common checked-bag limits.

ist2pst
Why the gap changes during the year
India keeps UTC+05:30 all year, but the US West Coast switches between Pacific Standard Time (UTC−8) and Pacific Daylight Time (UTC−7). Daylight saving starts on the second Sunday of March and ends on the first Sunday of November, so the difference is 13 hours 30 minutes in winter and 12 hours 30 minutes in summer.
Finding a good meeting slot
A 9:00 AM meeting in California is 9:30 PM or 10:30 PM in India, while 8:00 AM in India is the previous evening on the West Coast. Many distributed teams use early-morning Pacific / late-evening India slots. Always include both times and the date in invitations, because the calendar day often differs.
FAQ: Does it handle daylight saving?=Yes. The date you choose determines whether PST or PDT applies. || Is the date different?=Often yes – India is ahead, so evening in California is already the next morning in India.

utc2ist
What UTC is and why it matters
Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) is the global reference time used by aviation, servers, satellites and international events. GMT is often used as a synonym in everyday speech. Because UTC never changes for daylight saving, it is the safest way to record timestamps in logs, databases and APIs.
Reading timestamps correctly
ISO 8601 timestamps ending in “Z” (for example 2026-03-01T14:00:00Z) are in UTC. Add 5 hours 30 minutes to get India Standard Time: 14:00 UTC is 19:30 IST. When a timestamp has an explicit offset like +05:30 it is already local and needs no conversion.
FAQ: What is the offset?=IST is UTC+05:30 all year; India does not observe daylight saving. || Is GMT the same as UTC?=For everyday purposes yes; technically UTC is the precise atomic-clock standard.

c2f
Where Fahrenheit is still used
Most of the world measures temperature in Celsius, but the United States, some Caribbean nations and a few Pacific territories still use Fahrenheit for weather, cooking and medicine. If you read US recipes, weather apps or medical advice, converting quickly avoids mistakes – especially with oven temperatures.
Handy reference points
0 °C = 32 °F (freezing), 20 °C = 68 °F (room temperature), 37 °C = 98.6 °F (normal body temperature), 100 °C = 212 °F (boiling at sea level), and 180 °C ≈ 356 °F, the most common oven setting. The quick estimate “double it and add 30” is accurate within a few degrees for everyday weather.
FAQ: What is the formula?=°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. || How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?=°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.

utemp
Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin explained
Celsius is based on water freezing at 0° and boiling at 100°. Fahrenheit, used in the US, puts those points at 32° and 212°. Kelvin is the scientific absolute scale: 0 K is absolute zero, and one kelvin is the same size as one degree Celsius, so you simply add 273.15.
Common conversions
Oven: 200 °C ≈ 392 °F. Fever: 38 °C = 100.4 °F. Freezer: −18 °C ≈ 0 °F. Room temperature in science is often 298.15 K (25 °C). Note that −40 is the same in both Celsius and Fahrenheit.
FAQ: What formula is used?=°F = °C × 9/5 + 32 and K = °C + 273.15. || Can Kelvin be negative?=No. 0 K is absolute zero, the lowest possible temperature.

cm2in
Where the inch is still used
Even in metric countries, inches appear on TV and monitor sizes, phone screens, bicycle wheels, pipe fittings and denim waist sizes. Paper sizes differ too: A4 is 21 × 29.7 cm (8.27 × 11.69 in) while US Letter is 8.5 × 11 in (21.59 × 27.94 cm).
Quick estimates
Divide by 2.5 for a fast estimate, then adjust slightly up: 30 cm ≈ 11.8 in, 50 cm ≈ 19.7 in, 100 cm ≈ 39.4 in. For height, 170 cm is 5 ft 7 in and 180 cm is 5 ft 11 in.

umass
Mass units around the world
Most countries use grams and kilograms. The US uses ounces and pounds, the UK commonly uses stone for body weight, and precious metals use troy ounces (31.1 g) – different from the ordinary ounce (28.35 g). Some regions also have traditional units for gold and grain, which this converter includes.
Practical examples
A 500 g pack is 17.6 oz; a 2 kg bag of flour is 4.4 lb; 80 kg body weight is 12 st 8 lb. When shipping parcels, carriers often round up to the next whole kilogram or pound, so convert before you buy postage.

ist2est
Daylight saving and the time difference
India Standard Time is UTC+05:30 all year. US Eastern Time is UTC−5 (EST) in winter and UTC−4 (EDT) from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November. That makes India 10 h 30 min ahead in winter and 9 h 30 min ahead in summer.
Scheduling tips
Overlap is short: 8:00–10:00 AM in New York is 6:30–8:30 PM (summer) or 5:30–7:30 PM (winter) in India. Put both local times in every invitation and let calendar apps convert automatically to avoid missed calls.

ulength
Metric and imperial lengths
The metric system scales by powers of ten, which makes conversions inside it easy: 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, 1,000 m = 1 km. Imperial units – inches, feet, yards and miles – are still used in the US, and the UK uses miles for road distances. Since 1959 the inch is defined as exactly 2.54 cm.
Everyday references
A marathon is 42.195 km (26.2 mi). A football pitch is roughly 100–110 m long. A standard door is about 2 m (6 ft 8 in) tall. One nautical mile, used at sea and in aviation, is 1,852 m.

lakh2m
How the two numbering systems group digits
The international system groups digits in thousands: 1,000,000 is one million. The South Asian system groups the first three digits and then pairs: 10,00,000 is ten lakh – the same number. Business news, salaries and property prices in South Asia are often quoted in lakh and crore, so international readers need a quick translation.
Key equivalents
1 lakh = 100,000 (one hundred thousand). 10 lakh = 1 million. 1 crore = 10 million. 100 crore = 1 billion. So a figure of 250 crore equals 2.5 billion.
FAQ: How many lakh are in a million?=1 million = 10 lakh. || How many crore are in a billion?=1 billion = 100 crore.

upscale
Upscaling vs enlarging
Both make an image bigger. Upscaling targets a specific multiple (2×, 3×, 4×) to reach a resolution required by a platform or print size. Quality depends on the source: a sharp 1000 px photo upscaled 2× looks far better than a blurry thumbnail upscaled 4×.
Print size guide
For printing at 300 DPI, a 10 × 15 cm (4 × 6 in) print needs about 1200 × 1800 px; an A4 or Letter print needs about 2500 × 3500 px. Upscaling helps you reach these sizes, though it cannot add true detail that was never captured.

udata
Decimal vs binary units
Storage makers use decimal prefixes (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes), while Windows and some tools show binary values (1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes). That is why a “1 TB” drive shows about 931 GB in Windows – nothing is missing.
Bits, bytes and internet speed
Internet speeds are quoted in megabits per second (Mbit/s), file sizes in megabytes (MB). Divide by 8: a 100 Mbit/s connection downloads about 12.5 MB per second in ideal conditions, so a 1 GB file takes around 80 seconds.
FAQ: Why does my drive show less space?=Manufacturers use decimal units while many operating systems display binary units. || Is a megabit the same as a megabyte?=No. One byte is 8 bits, so 1 MB = 8 Mbit.

There are more than 38 local time offsets in use, including half-hour and 45-minute zones such as India (UTC+05:30) and Nepal (UTC+05:45). Getting small, smooth GIFs
GIF is an old format limited to 256 colours per frame, so file size grows quickly. The three settings that matter most are width, frame rate and length. A 480 px wide, 12 fps, 4-second clip is usually a few megabytes – fine for chats, docs and emails.
GIF or MP4?

mp32wav
When you need WAV
WAV is uncompressed PCM audio, the universal format for audio editors, DAWs, broadcasting systems, telephone on-hold systems and many hardware samplers. Editing a WAV avoids repeated MP3 encoding, which would add artefacts each time you save.
What to expect
A WAV file is roughly 10 MB per minute in stereo at 44.1 kHz, about ten times larger than a typical MP3. The sound will be identical to the MP3 you started with: converting cannot restore detail removed by MP3 compression.
FAQ: Which WAV format is produced?=16-bit PCM WAV, compatible with virtually all software and hardware. || Will it sound better than the MP3?=No, it sounds the same; the benefit is compatibility and lossless editing.

27	Auto crop: trim white margins
28	Auto crop renders each page on your device, finds the outermost non-white pixels, and sets the crop box just outside them plus the padding you choose (3 mm by default). With **Same crop box on every page** ticked, the tool uses the smallest margin found across all selected pages, so every page keeps the same size and content never jumps around when you flip pages – best for books and reports. Untick it to crop each page tightly to its own content, which suits figure collections and scanned receipts of different sizes.
29	Pages that are rotated in the file are left as they are in auto mode, and completely blank pages are skipped.

47	Is cropping permanent?
48	PDF cropping works by changing the page's visible area – the crop box, media box, trim box and bleed box are all set to the new size, so every viewer and printer shows the cropped page. The content outside the box is hidden, not erased, which keeps quality perfect and the operation lossless. If you need hidden content to be truly gone (for example, confidential notes in a margin), use [Redact PDF](/redact-pdf/) or [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf/) in *all content* mode afterwards.
49	Crop PDF to JPG
50	To get a cropped picture rather than a PDF – a figure for a presentation or a website – crop here first, then convert the result with [PDF to JPG](/pdf-to-jpg/) or [PDF to PNG](/pdf-to-png/). Because the crop box is respected, the images contain only the area you kept.
51	Crop vs resize
52	Cropping removes edges and makes the page smaller. [Resize PDF](/resize-pdf/) changes the paper size – for example, from Letter to A4 – by scaling the content to fit. If a document must be a specific paper size after cropping, crop first and then resize.
53	Tips for the best result
54	For books, keep *same crop box* on so pages line up; for mixed documents, turn it off.
55	Add 2–5 mm padding to avoid text touching the edge on e-readers.

57	Crop only the pages that need it by entering a range – covers often have different margins.
58	Check the first and last page after cropping: page numbers in the footer are content too and will be kept.

58	Scaling without distortion
59	Each page's content is placed as a vector form on the new page, scaled by the same factor horizontally and vertically, then centred. Text stays selectable and sharp; nothing is rasterised. If the original and target shapes differ – for example Letter to A4 – there will be slightly wider white space on two sides, which is better than stretching the content.
60	Printing a Letter PDF on A4 (and back)
61	Printing a US Letter document on A4 paper without resizing often cuts off the bottom lines or leaves uneven margins. Converting it to A4 here first gives a clean result on any printer. Similarly, convert A4 documents to Letter before sending them to North American printers and courts.
62	Resize pages vs reduce file size
63	'Resize PDF' can mean two things. This tool changes the *physical page dimensions* – for example from Letter to A4, or making a PDF with mixed page sizes uniform. If you want a smaller *file* in megabytes, use [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf/) or one of the target-size tools such as [Compress PDF to 1 MB](/compress-pdf-to-1mb/). Changing page size does not by itself make the file noticeably smaller, because the content is kept as vectors.
64	Mixed page sizes
65	Scans from different machines, merged documents and phone captures often produce PDFs where every page is a slightly different size, which looks messy and confuses printers. Resizing to one size centres every page's content on identical sheets, so the document prints consistently, binds neatly and looks professional when scrolled on screen.

72	Margins for binding and printing
73	Office printers cannot print to the very edge; a 5 mm margin keeps content safe. For ring or comb binding add 10–15 mm. Commercial printers need bleed rather than margin – see [Print-Ready PDF](/print-ready-pdf/). The margin setting here is applied on every side, and content is scaled down evenly to fit inside it.

78	Common problems and fixes
79	**"File is encrypted"** – remove the open password with Unlock PDF, then try again.
80	**The file is a cloud placeholder** – download it fully from OneDrive, iCloud or Drive first.
81	**A damaged or truncated download** – run it through [Repair PDF](/repair-pdf/).
82	**Very slow on a phone** – large scanned PDFs need lots of memory; close other tabs or split the file with [Split PDF](/split-pdf/) first.
83	**The download did not start** – some in-app browsers (Instagram, Facebook) block downloads; open the page in Chrome or Safari.
84	Combine it with other PDF tools
85	Most jobs take more than one step, and every Fileopia tool works the same way, so you can chain them without uploading anything. 86	Privacy
87	Everything happens inside your browser tab with pdf-lib and PDF.js. The PDF is read from your disk into memory, processed there and saved straight back to your device.

29	Flowing text or exact layout – which should I pick?
30	Choose **Flowing text** when you want to edit the document: rewrite paragraphs, fix a CV, update a report or copy sections into another file. Lines are joined into paragraphs, headings are detected from font size and weight, multi-column pages are read in the right order and ruled tables become real Word tables. Text reflows naturally as you type.
31	Choose **Exact layout** when the document must look identical to the PDF – forms, brochures, certificates or anything with a designed layout. Every line is placed at its original position, so the page looks the same in Word, but editing large blocks of text is less convenient because each line lives in its own box.

34	A scanned PDF contains pictures of pages rather than text, so an ordinary converter produces an empty Word file or a document full of images.

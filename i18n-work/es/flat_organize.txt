40	Sorting a scanned document
41	Double-sided documents scanned on a single-sided feeder are a classic problem: you get all the front pages first, then all the back pages in reverse. To sort a PDF like this, use Reverse order on a copy containing the back pages, then merge and interleave – or simply drag the pages into place here if the document is short. 58	Blank pages for double-sided printing
59	When you print double-sided, each chapter should usually start on a right-hand (odd) page. If a section ends on an odd page, insert a blank page after it so the next section starts on the front of a new sheet. The blank page uses the size of your first page, so it matches the document. Blank pages also help when you need room for handwritten notes or a stamp.
60	Quality and what is preserved
61	Pages are copied, not re-rendered. Text stays selectable and searchable, images keep their original resolution and fonts remain embedded, so the organized PDF looks exactly like the source. Links and form fields on copied pages are kept by most viewers; document-level bookmarks that pointed to the old page order are not rebuilt, so add new ones with [Edit bookmarks](/pdf-bookmark-editor/) if you need them.

There is no page limit and no file-size cap other than your device's memory.

66	Worked example: fixing a jumbled 12-page report
67	Say a colleague scanned a 12-page report and the pages came out as 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12, with page 8 upside down. Drop the file and the thumbnails make the problem obvious at a glance. Drag the block of pages 4–6 so they sit after page 3 – on a computer, drag each thumbnail onto the position you want; on a phone, tap the ← button on page 4 three times, then repeat for 5 and 6. Tap ↻ twice on page 8 to turn it 180°. Finally, if the report needs a blank back page so it prints evenly double-sided, select page 12 and press *Insert blank page*. Save, and you have a clean, correctly ordered PDF in under a minute without installing anything.
68	Tips for working quickly

73	Deleted pages stay visible with a ✕ until you save, so you can change your mind; tap again to restore.
74	Common questions about page order in PDFs
75	A PDF stores pages as an ordered list, and reorganizing simply writes that list in a new order. That is why the operation is lossless and fast. Page labels (the custom numbering some viewers show, such as *i, ii, iii* for front matter) are not changed, and printed page numbers that are part of the page content stay as they were – if you move page 7 to the front, it still shows *7* in its footer. To renumber, run [Add page numbers](/add-page-numbers/) after organizing, ideally on a version without the old printed numbers.

Rotate, delete and select buttons are large enough to tap accurately.

43	How the AI background remover works
44	The tool uses U²-Net, a neural network designed for 'salient object detection' – finding the thing in a photo that a person would naturally look at. The image is scaled to 320×320 pixels and passed through the network, which outputs a probability for every pixel of being foreground. That low-resolution mask is then scaled back up to the full size of your photo with smooth interpolation, so the final cut-out keeps the full detail of the original image, not the size the network saw.
45	With soft edges, the mask is lightly feathered so semi-transparent pixels at the boundary blend smoothly into any new background – important for hair, fur and motion blur. With sharp edges, every pixel is either fully kept or fully removed, which gives crisp outlines for boxes, bottles and electronics.
46	Because the model runs on your device, speed depends on your hardware: a modern laptop processes a photo in one to three seconds, a phone in a few seconds more.

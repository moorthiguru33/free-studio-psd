56	What is kept
57	Extracted pages are exact copies: selectable text, fonts, vector graphics and images at full resolution. Page size and rotation are preserved. Comments and annotations that belong to a page travel with it in most cases. Bookmarks from the original document are not copied, because they would point to pages that may no longer exist.

62	Worked example: pulling three invoices out of a statement
63	Imagine a 40-page supplier statement where you only need the invoices on pages 6, 14 and 15. Drop the PDF, type *6,14-15* in the range box and press *Select range* – the three thumbnails light up. To file each invoice separately, tick *Save each selected page as a separate PDF*. Press Extract, and you get a ZIP containing *statement-page6.pdf*, *statement-page14.pdf* and *statement-page15.pdf*. Without the tick, you would get one three-page PDF instead. The whole job takes a few seconds and the statement is never uploaded anywhere.

80	Extracting pages with comments or form fields
81	Annotations such as highlights, sticky notes and drawn markup are stored on the page they belong to and are carried over to the extracted file in most viewers. Fillable form fields are copied with their pages, but if a form uses scripts that reference fields on other pages, those scripts may stop working once the pages are separated. If you need a fixed, non-editable copy, run [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf/) before extracting.
82	Reasons to extract rather than share the whole file
83	**Privacy** – send only the page someone needs, not the full document with personal details on other pages.
84	**Size** – a two-page extract is far smaller than a 300-page manual, so it fits in e-mail and chat limits.
85	**Clarity** – reviewers see exactly the section they must sign or comment on.
86	**Filing** – store each receipt, certificate or form under its own name in your records.
87	Select odd or even pages
88	The *Odd* and *Even* buttons select every odd-numbered (1, 3, 5…) or even-numbered (2, 4, 6…) page in one click. That is useful for separating the fronts and backs of a double-sided scan, or for pulling out only the right-hand pages of a book scan. Combine them with typed ranges and thumbnail clicks to fine-tune the selection.
89	Extract pages online vs desktop software
90	Paid desktop PDF editors offer page extraction, and many online services do too – usually by uploading your file to their servers and sometimes with hourly limits for free users. Here, the PDF is opened in your browser and the new file is created on your device, with no limits on how often you use it. That makes it fast for large files on slow connections, and safe for confidential documents.

39	What is a HEIC file?
40	HEIC is Apple's name for images stored in the HEIF container using HEVC (H.265) compression – the same technology used for 4K video. Apple made it the default camera format in iOS 11 in 2017 because it stores photos at roughly half the size of an equivalent JPG with the same visual quality. A HEIC file can also hold extra data: depth maps for Portrait mode, multiple images for bursts and Live Photos, HDR information and transparency.
41	The catch is compatibility. HEVC is covered by patents and licences, so support outside Apple's ecosystem arrived slowly. Windows needs an extension from the Microsoft Store to show HEIC thumbnails, many web forms only accept JPG or PNG, and some older Android phones and photo editors cannot open them at all.

Both work, but they are slow for large batches and easy to get wrong – this converter handles a whole folder in one drop and gives you a ZIP, with the same result on a Mac, a Windows PC or a Chromebook.

64	How to stop your iPhone saving HEIC
65	If you would rather your iPhone always took JPG photos, open Settings → Camera → Formats and choose 'Most Compatible'. New photos will be saved as JPG (and videos as H.264). Photos you already took stay HEIC – convert those here.
66	Alternatively, keep HEIC for storage and let the iPhone convert automatically when sharing: Settings → Photos → Transfer to Mac or PC → Automatic. Note that this only applies to some transfer methods; AirDrop, e-mail and cloud uploads may still send HEIC files.

88	Why HEIC uploads fail
89	If a website rejects your photo with 'unsupported file type' or shows a broken preview, it almost always means the site expects JPG or PNG and you selected a HEIC file. Some sites convert automatically when you upload from Safari on the iPhone itself, but the same photo copied to a Windows PC or uploaded from Google Drive stays HEIC. Converting to JPG first is the reliable fix. If the site also says 'maximum 2 MB' or 'under 500 KB', run the JPG through [Compress Image](/compress-image/) with a target size.
90	HEIC photos on Google Photos, iCloud and OneDrive
91	Cloud services handle HEIC differently. iCloud Photos keeps the original HEIC files and converts on download only in some situations. Google Photos stores HEIC files as uploaded and shows them in the browser, but downloading gives you the HEIC again. OneDrive and Dropbox store whatever you upload. If you download a set of photos from any of these and need JPGs, drop the downloaded files here – the conversion happens locally, so you are not uploading your photos to yet another service.
92	Is HEIC better than JPG?
93	For storage, yes: HEIC keeps the same visual quality at about half the size, supports 10-bit colour and HDR, and can hold depth data for Portrait mode editing. For sharing, JPG is still better because every device, program and website can open it. A sensible workflow is to keep your originals in HEIC on the phone or in iCloud and convert copies to JPG only when you need to send, print or upload them.
94	Converting from JPG back to HEIC does not restore anything – it only re-encodes the already compressed JPG. Always keep the HEIC original if you might want the best quality later.

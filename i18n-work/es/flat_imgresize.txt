43	Pixels, centimetres and DPI explained
44	Digital images are measured in pixels. Physical sizes such as centimetres or inches only make sense together with a resolution, measured in dots (pixels) per inch. At 300 DPI – the usual standard for photo prints – one inch is 300 pixels and one centimetre is about 118 pixels. So a 35×45 mm passport photo at 300 DPI is 413×531 pixels, and a 4×6 inch print is 1200×1800 pixels.

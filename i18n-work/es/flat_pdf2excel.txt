33	How table detection works
34	For digital PDFs the converter reads every text fragment with its exact position. Fragments on the same baseline become a row; consistent gaps between fragments become column boundaries. Ruled lines, when present, help confirm the grid. Numbers with thousands separators, currency symbols, percentages and negative values in brackets are recognised and stored as numeric cells so formulas work immediately.
35	For scanned pages the page is rendered at 300 DPI, OCR returns every word with its bounding box, and the same row/column logic is applied. The result screen tells you how many pages were read with OCR so you know which ones to double-check.

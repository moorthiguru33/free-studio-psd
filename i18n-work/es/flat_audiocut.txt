100	Volume and loudness
101	The volume control multiplies the signal: 200% doubles the amplitude (about +6 dB), 50% halves it. Raising a quiet recording too far can cause clipping – a harsh distortion when peaks exceed the maximum level. If you hear crackling at loud moments after boosting, try a smaller increase. For consistent podcast loudness across episodes, set the volume so the loudest parts of the waveform nearly, but not completely, fill the height.

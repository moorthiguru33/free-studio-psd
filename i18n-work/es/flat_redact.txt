22	Redact a PDF for free – real removal, not a black box on top
23	Redaction means permanently removing sensitive information – names, account numbers, addresses, signatures – before you share a document. The most common mistake is drawing black rectangles in an ordinary PDF editor: the text underneath is still there and can be copied out. This tool does real redaction. Every page that contains a redaction is rebuilt as an image with the boxes burned in, so the hidden text, its font data and any selectable layer underneath are gone. Pages without redactions are copied untouched, and document metadata is cleared.
24	You can redact by **searching** for words or phrases (comma-separated, e.g. *John Smith, 4111 1111*), by automatically finding **all e-mail addresses** and **phone numbers**, or by **drawing boxes** over anything – photos, signatures, stamps, handwriting – on the page preview.

31	Black or white redaction?
32	Black boxes are the convention for legal and government documents because they make it obvious that something was removed. White boxes (sometimes called *white-out redaction*) blend into the page and are used when a clean look matters, for example in published examples or templates. Both remove the content equally.

24	What does it mean to flatten a PDF?
25	Flattening merges interactive or layered parts of a PDF – fillable form fields, check boxes, drop-downs, comments, stamps and drawings – into the page itself. The document looks the same, but those elements stop being separate editable objects. A flattened form shows the answers as ordinary page content that cannot be changed by clicking into a field, and it prints and displays the same in every viewer.
26	People flatten PDFs before sending completed applications, contracts or tax forms, before archiving, when a portal rejects files with form fields, and when filled-in answers disappear or look different in another app.

47	After flattening, form answers can no longer be changed by typing into fields. Bear in mind that any PDF can still be modified with an editor – flattening prevents accidental and casual changes, not determined tampering. For stronger protection, add a password with editing disabled using [Protect PDF](/protect-pdf/), and for proof of integrity use a certificate-based digital signature.
48	Does flattening decrease quality?
49	*Form fields* mode does not touch the rest of the page, so quality is identical. *All content* mode renders pages at about 144 dpi with high JPEG quality, which looks sharp on screen and in normal printing, but text becomes part of an image and the file can grow for text-heavy documents. Use it when appearance matters more than size or searchability.
50	Flattening a large PDF
51	Form-field flattening is quick even for long documents. All-content mode renders every page, so a 200-page file takes longer and the output may be larger; you can run [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf/) afterwards. A progress counter shows which page is being processed.
52	Why answers vanish without flattening
53	Some viewers, mobile apps and print drivers do not display form field values correctly, especially when fields use custom fonts or scripts. The person receiving your form may see empty boxes. Flattening draws the values into the page so everyone sees the same thing.
54	Flatten PDF online vs desktop software
55	Desktop editors can flatten through print-to-PDF or preflight tools, often in paid versions. Online services usually require uploading your completed form – including personal details like ID numbers and addresses. This tool flattens on your device, for free.

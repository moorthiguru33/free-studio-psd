31	Two layouts: reflowed paragraphs or exact lines
32	Plain text has no formatting of its own, so the converter offers two ways to lay it out. *Reflow paragraphs* treats blank lines as paragraph breaks and wraps the words to the page width in a proportional font – ideal for notes, letters, essays, e-mails and exported chat logs, because the PDF reads like a normal document. *Keep exact lines – monospace* keeps every line break and space exactly as typed in a Courier-style font, which is what you want for source code, configuration files, server logs, ASCII art and column-aligned tables. Lines that are wider than the page are wrapped onto the next line instead of being cut off, so nothing is lost.
33	Markdown and RTF are converted with their formatting
34	Markdown files are rendered with GitHub-flavoured Markdown: headings, bold and italic text, bullet and numbered lists, task-list check boxes, block quotes, code blocks, horizontal rules, tables and images embedded as data URIs. RTF files from WordPad, TextEdit and older word processors keep their paragraphs, bold text and special characters. If you want more control over Markdown output, the dedicated [Markdown to PDF](/markdown-to-pdf/) tool has extra options.

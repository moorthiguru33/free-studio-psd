33	Why spacing or order can look odd
34	PDFs store text as positioned fragments, not paragraphs. The extractor rebuilds lines and paragraphs from their positions. Multi-column layouts, tables, footnotes and sidebars may come out in a different order than you read them, and hyphenated line breaks stay hyphenated. Tables are best extracted with [PDF to Excel](/pdf-to-excel/) or [PDF to CSV](/pdf-to-csv/).

47	Common problems and fixes
48	**"File is encrypted"** – remove the open password with Unlock PDF, then try again.
49	**The file is a cloud placeholder** – download it fully from OneDrive, iCloud or Drive first.
50	**A damaged or truncated download** – run it through [Repair PDF](/repair-pdf/).
51	**Very slow on a phone** – large scanned PDFs need lots of memory; close other tabs or split the file with [Split PDF](/split-pdf/) first.
52	**The download did not start** – some in-app browsers (Instagram, Facebook) block downloads; open the page in Chrome or Safari.
53	Combine it with other PDF tools
54	Most jobs take more than one step, and every Fileopia tool works the same way, so you can chain them without uploading anything. A typical workflow: scan or convert the pages, [merge](/merge-pdf/) them into one file, put them in order with [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf/), make scanned text searchable with OCR, add [page numbers](/add-page-numbers/), and finally [compress](/compress-pdf/) the result for e-mail. Each step downloads a new file and leaves your original untouched, so you can always go back and try different settings. Tip: give each output a clear name such as *contract-signed-compressed.pdf* so you can tell the versions apart later.
55	Privacy
56	Everything happens inside your browser tab with pdf-lib and PDF.js. The PDF is read from your disk into memory, processed there and saved straight back to your device. It is never uploaded, stored or logged, and the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded – which makes it suitable for contracts, medical records, HR files, bank statements and anything else you would not e-mail to a stranger.

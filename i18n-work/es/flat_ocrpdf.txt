25	Make scans searchable and copyable
26	A scanned PDF is a stack of pictures: you cannot search it, copy a sentence from it or have a screen reader read it aloud. OCR (optical character recognition) reads the letters in those pictures and adds an invisible text layer exactly over them. The page looks the same, but now Ctrl+F finds words, text can be selected and copied, and the document can be indexed by your computer or a document management system.

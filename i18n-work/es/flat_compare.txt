25	Compare two PDF documents for differences
26	Compare PDF shows exactly what changed between two versions of a document. Drop the original and the revised PDF, and the text of both is lined up side by side: deleted lines are highlighted red on the left, added lines green on the right, and changed lines yellow with the exact words struck through or inserted. A summary shows the number of deletions, additions and changes, and you can download the comparison as an HTML report to share or archive.
27	It is the quickest way to check a contract redline, confirm that only the agreed clauses were changed in a new policy, review edits to a thesis, or spot differences between two invoices or quotes.
28	How to compare two PDF files
29	Drop both PDFs (original first, then revised).
30	Press Compare.
31	Read the side-by-side table: red = removed, green = added, yellow = changed.
32	Download the HTML report if you need to keep or send it.
33	What is compared
34	The tool compares the **text** of the documents line by line, then word by word within changed lines, and shows the page number of every line. That makes it ideal for contracts, reports, policies and other text documents. Formatting changes (font, colour, layout) and image changes are not detected, and scanned PDFs need [OCR PDF](/ocr-pdf/) first so they contain text.

46	Can I compare a PDF and a Word document?
47	Convert the Word file to PDF first with [Word to PDF](/word-to-pdf/), then compare the two PDFs. Alternatively convert the PDF to Word with [PDF to Word](/pdf-to-word/) and use Word's built-in compare feature.
48	Tips for accurate comparisons
49	Compare documents produced the same way – two exports from Word compare more cleanly than a Word export against a scan.
50	Re-flowed text (a paragraph that moves to a new page) still matches, because comparison follows the text, not the page.
51	Headers and footers appear as lines too; changed page numbers can show as small changes.
52	For long documents, use your browser's find (Ctrl+F) in the report to jump to a clause.
53	Compare PDF online vs desktop software
54	Professional PDF suites and dedicated comparison apps offer visual comparison in paid plans, and online tools typically upload both documents. Here, both files are read in your browser and never leave your device – important when comparing confidential contracts.

66	Tips for accurate comparisons
67	Compare text-based PDFs for word-level results; run [OCR](/ocr-pdf/) on scans first. If pages were inserted, differences after that point may be shifted – compare sections with [Extract pages](/extract-pages-from-pdf/) when needed.
68	What the comparison does not cover
69	Text is compared word by word; changes to images, colours, fonts and layout are not highlighted. For visual changes, open both files side by side, or convert pages with [PDF to JPG](/pdf-to-jpg/) and inspect them. Metadata and hidden content are not compared either. Use it as a fast first check, then review key clauses yourself.
70	Privacy
71	Everything happens inside your browser tab with pdf-lib and PDF.js. The PDF is read from your disk into memory, processed there and saved straight back to your device. It is never uploaded, stored or logged, and the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded – which makes it suitable for contracts, medical records, HR files, bank statements and anything else you would not e-mail to a stranger.

48	Raster to SVG tracing
49	Choosing SVG traces the picture into coloured vector shapes. Use 2 colours for black-and-white logos and Cricut or Silhouette cut files, 8–16 for flat illustrations, and 32–64 for detailed artwork. Photos do not trace well – the result is a posterised look. See [PNG to SVG](/png-to-svg/) for tips.

82	Common problems and fixes
83	**The file will not open** – check it is a real image and not a shortcut or a cloud placeholder; download it fully from iCloud, Google Photos or OneDrive first.
84	**The result looks blurry** – start from the original, not a thumbnail sent through WhatsApp or Messenger, which recompress photos heavily.
85	**Colours look slightly different** – wide-gamut (Display P3) photos are shown in sRGB, the standard for the web; this is normal and consistent across devices.
86	**The download does not appear on iPhone** – look in Files → Downloads, or long-press the preview and choose Save to Photos.
87	**A big batch is slow** – very large photos use a lot of memory; close other tabs or process 20–30 images at a time.

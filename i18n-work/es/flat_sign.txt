37	Electronic signature vs digital signature
38	These two terms are often confused. An **electronic signature** is any electronic indication that a person agrees to a document – a drawn or typed signature, a scanned image, even ticking a box. That is what this tool creates. In many countries electronic signatures are legally recognised for most everyday agreements: in the United States under the ESIGN Act and UETA, and in the European Union as 'simple electronic signatures' under the eIDAS regulation.
39	A **digital signature** is a specific technology: a cryptographic seal based on a certificate issued to you, which proves who signed and that the file has not been changed since. Some documents – certain government filings, notarised deeds or 'qualified' signatures under eIDAS – require that level. If a recipient specifically asks for a certificate-based digital signature, use the tool or service they specify.

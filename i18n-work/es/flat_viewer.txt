51	Why read PDFs in the browser?
52	Desktop PDF software is often large, asks for updates and sign-ins, and opens files with features (scripts, forms, plug-ins) you rarely need. A browser-based reader is sandboxed by the browser's security model, starts instantly and works the same on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, Android and iOS. Because Fileopia renders the file locally with Mozilla's PDF.js engine – the same engine behind Firefox's built-in viewer – confidential documents never leave your device.
53	Searching a PDF
54	Type a word or phrase; the hit list shows each occurrence with the surrounding text and page number.
55	Click a hit to jump to it; matches are highlighted on the page.
56	Search is case-insensitive and works across the whole document.

58	Reading comfortably on a phone
59	Use *fit width* so lines fill the screen, then scroll vertically. Pinch-zoom works as usual. Night mode reduces glare at bedtime. For books, a reflowable format is easier on small screens – convert with [PDF to EPUB](/pdf-to-epub/), or listen instead with [PDF to Speech](/pdf-to-speech/).
60	Saving only some pages
61	Type page numbers and ranges such as 1-3, 8, 10-12 and press *Save selected pages* to get a smaller PDF with just those pages – handy for sending one chapter, a single invoice from a statement or the signature page of a contract. For more page operations see [Split PDF](/split-pdf/), [Extract Pages](/extract-pdf-pages/) and [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf/).
62	File types and limits
63	The reader opens standard PDFs from version 1.0 to 2.0, including files with forms, annotations, layers and embedded fonts. Very large scans (hundreds of megabytes) depend on your device's memory; a modern phone comfortably reads files of 50–100 MB. Password-protected PDFs need the password – remove it permanently with [Unlock PDF](/unlock-pdf/) if you are allowed to.

66	Opening PDFs from e-mail and cloud storage
67	On a phone, tap the drop area and choose *Browse* or *Files* to pick a PDF saved from Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp, Google Drive, iCloud Drive or OneDrive. Cloud files must be downloaded to the device first; files that are still "online only" placeholders cannot be read. On a computer, drag the attachment from your downloads folder onto the page.
68	Accessibility

Zoom up to large sizes for low vision, use night mode to reduce glare, and have pages read aloud with [PDF to Speech](/pdf-to-speech/). Tagged PDFs keep their reading order in the text layer.

74	Common questions about opening PDFs
75	**"Failed to load PDF"** – the file may be incomplete or damaged; try [Repair PDF](/repair-pdf/).
76	**Blank or grey pages** – very complex vector drawings can take a few seconds; wait or zoom out.
77	**Strange characters** – the PDF uses unembedded fonts; text is still searchable if a text layer exists.
78	**Sideways pages** – rotate them permanently with Rotate PDF.
79	**Huge file on a phone** – compress it first or save only the pages you need.
80	Comparing PDF readers
81	Adobe Acrobat Reader is the reference desktop app and supports every PDF feature, including XFA forms and certificate signatures, but it is large and pushes a subscription. Browser built-in viewers in Chrome, Edge and Firefox are quick but have limited search lists and no page export. Fileopia sits in between: instant, private, with a hit list, night mode and page export, and nothing to install.

90	Business and legal documents
91	Contracts, invoices, statements, tenders and HR policies often contain personal or commercially sensitive data, and many companies forbid uploading them to online converters. A local reader avoids that problem entirely: the file is read from your disk into the browser tab and disappears when you close it. Use search to check clauses quickly – "termination", "indemnity", "payment" – and save the relevant pages as an extract for a colleague.
92	Older and unusual PDFs
93	Files produced by old scanners, fax software and legacy publishing systems sometimes use rare compression or fonts. PDF.js supports nearly all standard filters (Flate, LZW, DCT/JPEG, JBIG2, JPEG 2000, CCITT fax) and Type 1, TrueType, OpenType and CID fonts, so even decades-old archives usually display correctly. If a page looks wrong, try [Repair PDF](/repair-pdf/), which rebuilds the file structure.
94	Printing from the reader
95	To print, open the PDF in your browser's built-in viewer or download the selected pages and print them from there; choose *Actual size* to keep the original scale, or *Fit* for documents with wide margins. Printing only the pages you need saves paper and toner, especially with long manuals and statements where only a few pages matter. For double-sided booklets use Create Booklet PDF, and for large wall charts use PDF Poster. Duplex printers can print the extract on both sides automatically; choose *flip on long edge* for portrait documents so the back pages are not upside down, and check the preview before sending a long job to a shared office printer.

100	Common problems and fixes
101	**"File is encrypted"** – remove the open password with Unlock PDF, then try again.
102	**The file is a cloud placeholder** – download it fully from OneDrive, iCloud or Drive first.
103	**A damaged or truncated download** – run it through [Repair PDF](/repair-pdf/).
104	**Very slow on a phone** – large scanned PDFs need lots of memory; close other tabs or split the file with [Split PDF](/split-pdf/) first.
105	**The download did not start** – some in-app browsers (Instagram, Facebook) block downloads; open the page in Chrome or Safari.
106	Combine it with other PDF tools
107	Most jobs take more than one step, and every Fileopia tool works the same way, so you can chain them without uploading anything. A typical workflow: scan or convert the pages, [merge](/merge-pdf/) them into one file, put them in order with [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf/), make scanned text searchable with OCR, add [page numbers](/add-page-numbers/), and finally [compress](/compress-pdf/) the result for e-mail. Each step downloads a new file and leaves your original untouched, so you can always go back and try different settings. Tip: give each output a clear name such as *contract-signed-compressed.pdf* so you can tell the versions apart later.
108	Privacy
109	Everything happens inside your browser tab with pdf-lib and PDF.js. The PDF is read from your disk into memory, processed there and saved straight back to your device. It is never uploaded, stored or logged, and the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded – which makes it suitable for contracts, medical records, HR files, bank statements and anything else you would not e-mail to a stranger.

53	How strong is a watermark?
54	A watermark is drawn onto the page as text or an image. It is a clear visual statement of ownership or status and deters casual misuse, but a determined person with a PDF editor can remove objects from a page. For stronger protection combine a tiled watermark with [Protect PDF](/protect-pdf/) and disable editing and copying, or flatten the document with [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf/).
